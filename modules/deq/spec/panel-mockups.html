BMO DEQ Panel Options

Three layouts for the 12-band dynamic EQ, drawn in the suite's own panel vocabulary and set beside BMO EQ as it renders today. Every panel is the common 740 px tall: a 28 px header, a 24 px preset bar and the 688 px content area. Widths are multiples of 20, as modules/AGENTS.md asks.

  • Band 6 selected: a dynamic bell
  • Bands 3, 6, 8 and 9 dynamic, with range shown
  • Band 12 off
  • Curves computed by the DEQ engine, serial, 48 kHz

Shared by all three

  • The output section is the standard one from modules/AGENTS.md: a rule, a switch row and the OUTPUT trim, on the same lines as every other module in a rack. There's no input trim.
  • The teal is the module's accent: header bar, band caps and the lit switches. Azure stays the utility colour, and gain reduction uses the suite's meterGr.
  • DEQ and AUTO are placeholders for the switch row. AUTO would be output gain compensation, which isn't specified yet.
  • Russo One stands in for TG Minerva Black. The licensed faces can't go on a shared page, so letter widths here run a little wide.

Still open, whichever layout wins

  • Which 32 parameters take the rack's automation lanes. These layouts assume every band's full set: shape, frequency, gain, Q, placement, M/S amount, dynamics on, threshold, range, ratio, attack and release.
  • Whether numeric readouts, new to the suite, belong on DEQ or stay off it like BMO EQ's.
  • Light-plate versions. These are drawn on the dark plate the snapshot tool renders by default.
