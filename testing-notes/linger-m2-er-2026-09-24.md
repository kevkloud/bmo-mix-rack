## Measured — 11 §6's ER block, all green

`reverb_dsp_tests`, 48 kHz unless stated, ER-only condition (REVERB off, ER
0 dB, MIX 100 %, hi-cut 20 kHz, DENSITY 0, VARIATION 0):

- **Tables re-derive.** All six printed tables match `imagesource::table` on
  their recorded geometry and seed to the printed precision, and each passes
  the audit: separation ≥ 0.9 ms, no two gaps within 2 %, no tap above
  −15.3 dB, Kuttruff's ceiling in 2–20 ms, first two taps inside |pan| 0.25,
  a tap inside 5 ms, the three flamming rules.
- **The engine plays the tables.** Every core tap of every type arrives
  within ±1 sample and ±0.2 dB of its row (DC-sum gain, since the band poles
  have unity DC gain; the peak alone is attenuated by the pole). Times in ms
  within 0.1 ms at 44.1 / 96 / 192 kHz.
- **Mono, γ per VARIATION**, Room, over the span: 0.994 / 0.814 / 0.587 /
  0.382 / 0.217 / 0.101 / 0.000 for 0–6. Monotone, all ≥ 0, position 6 is
  zero to 1e−8 and its mono sum carries exactly one channel pair's energy.
- **Flamming on the rendered Room IR**: nothing after 25 ms above −12 dB re
  the energy at 25 ms; no 5 ms window rises after the peak (a window under
  −30 dB re the peak counts as empty); ≥ 50 % of the energy before 30 ms.
- **ER-only**: energy after span + 5 ms is > 60 dB down; the last tap is
  quieter than the one before and ≥ 5 ms after it.
- **DENSITY sweep 0–100 % in 5 % steps**: energy constant within
  **0.034 dB** (asserted ±0.2); every sample finite; a 0 → 100 % jump has a
  step ratio of 1.0003 against steady state; pulse rate at the top
  **31 000 /s** (asserted ≥ 2000).
- **ER HI-CUT** at 2 / 4 / 8 / 16 kHz: the design is −3.01 dB at its corner
  (exact one-pole), and the running filter matches the design against the
  open pole to 1e−4 dB; peak cross-correlation lag 0 ± 1.
- **Level laws**: ER at −6 / −12 / −24 moves the IR energy by exactly that
  ±0.1 dB; −40 is off (> 100 dB down); OUTPUT −12 trims −12; MIX 50 % with
  the wet faders off nulls against dry to 1e−4 **under the provisional law**.
- **Block size** 1 / 16 / 32 / 64 / 127 / 512 / 2048: bit-identical IR.
- **Parameter changes** (1 kHz sine, steady): step ratios TYPE 1.0002, SIZE
  1.0002, ER MODE 1.0002, VARIATION 1.0002 — no click.
- **Energy / Blend**: finite, and within 1 dB of Taps' energy (−0.29 / +0.03).
- **NaN / silence**: squares, DC, denormal input and silence at every type
  and both SIZE ends, VARIATION 0 and 6, DENSITY 0 and 100: all finite;
  after `reset()` zeros in gives zeros out.
- **Mono bus** within 0.003 dB of one stereo channel.

## Measured, and recorded rather than asserted

- **ER-only magnitude, 1/3-octave smoothed, 200 Hz–10 kHz, tilt removed**,
  Room, ripple peak to peak: **7.1 dB at DENSITY 0 %, 8.4 dB at 50 %,
  13.0 dB at 100 %**. 11 §6 asks for ±3 dB. No sparse cluster meets that
  under 1/3-octave smoothing at 200 Hz — the band is 46 Hz wide and any two
  taps a few ms apart comb wider than that — and with the diffuser on, its
  own short delays comb above 1 kHz. **The rule needs re-specifying by the
  owner** (octave smoothing, or a band above 1 kHz), and the listening pass
  hears what the number means. The tilt itself (−0.8 to −0.5 dB/octave) is
  the order-banded poles doing what 10 §3 asks.
- **Early lateral fraction at the default VARIATION 2**: ISO 3382-1's
  figure, direct sound included, is **0.010**; the ER bus's own lateral
  fraction is **0.204** (asserted 0.10–0.35). With every tap held at or under
  −15.3 dB the cluster is a tenth of the direct energy and the ISO figure
  cannot reach 0.10 whatever the bearings do. The test asserts the bus's
  figure and prints both.

## What is only reasoned about

- The tables' geometry: listener 0.86 of the way to the back wall, source
  nearly ahead of it, so the back-wall reflection lands inside 10 ms near
  centre. Chamber, Hall, Cavern, Plate and Ambience differ in β, window and
  a few positions, all CALIBRATE, claiming only the type list's ordering.
- The infill contour: a least-squares line in dB against time through the
  core taps.
- The VARIATION scheme (spread 0.12–1.0, split 0–0.92, offsets 0.6–2 ms) and
  the diffuser's rulers and stage thresholds: every one is CALIBRATE.

## How it was verified

Every figure above came from a build that exited 0, checked before ctest ran
(`cmake --build ... > log 2>&1; echo EXIT=$?`, never piped). Two bugs were
found by the tests and not by reading: the spec's own density ramp silences
the core taps at DENSITY 0 with θ at 0, and the test harness's click helper
ran 128 samples past its buffers (heap corruption, exit 0xC0000374). Four
diffuser normalisers were tried before the exact one; each was measured, not
argued. The installed VST3 folder was checked by date after every full build
and did not move.

What the table says, plainly:

- **Reverb plug D has no sparse early phase at all.** Its echo density is
  0.6 within 10 ms of onset and its channels are decorrelated from the first
  millisecond. Reference B's "smooth diffuse onset" is that. Our DENSITY 100
  reaches the same NED (0.92) and the same γ (≈0) — the bridge's top end is
  where the Reference-B character lives, and its bottom end (NED 0.1, γ 0.99)
  is the Reference-A positional cluster. That is what the bridge was for.
- **Every reference is flatter than we are.** Octave-smoothed ripple about
  the tilt: 0.8 dB (Reverb plug D), 2.6 dB (Reverb plug G), 6.0 dB (Reverb plug E, which is
  simply dark: −9.7 dB at 8 kHz). Ours is 4.3–6.9 dB, and the shape is a
  −3 dB trough at 250–500 Hz against 1 kHz from the strongest tap pairs
  combing (7.19 and 11.31 ms are 4.1 ms apart: a 243 Hz comb). A dense
  onset has no such pairs. This is the number behind the flatness decision.
- **Our ER-only cluster ends at 47 ms** (99.9 % of energy) at Room 12 m; the
  references' energy keeps arriving for a second or more, which is the tail
  M3 owes.

The same octave-smoothed figure for our engine from the test suite, for the
decision: ripple about the tilt **4.2 / 5.1 / 7.7 dB** at DENSITY 0 / 50 /
100 %, raw 5.1 / 5.3 / 9.1 dB, 250 Hz–8 kHz.

What the tables say, plainly:

- **Reference A's early reflections are decorrelated; ours are not.** γ
  over 0–80 ms is −0.18 to +0.20 on every Reference A type, and on every Vendor 4
  unit too. Linger at each type's defaults (VARIATION 2) measures 0.36 to
  0.59. This is the biggest measured difference, and it is a choice about
  defaults, not a limit of the engine: Room at VARIATION 6 or at DENSITY 100
  reaches about 0 (the table above). It is worth hearing whether the
  default should sit wider.
- **Reference A's early reflections are darker.** The tilt is −1.2 to
  −3.7 dB/octave against our −0.2 to −0.9. Reference A seems to low-pass its
  reflections by default, which is the ER hi-cut's job in Linger.
- **Reference A is not flatter than we are.** Its ER ripple about the tilt
  runs from 1.9 dB (type 5) to 9.5 dB (type 6), and type 3 is 5.6 dB. The
  6 dB flatness rule sits inside the range the reference itself shows. The
  "every reference is flatter" point above holds for Reverb plug D,
  Reverb plug F and Reverb plug O (0.4–0.8 dB), not for Reference A.
- **Reference A's early reflections last much longer at the small sizes.**
  At Room, Chamber and Ambience, Reference A's ER-only energy takes 141–327 ms to
  arrive; ours takes 40–93 ms. At Hall and Cavern the two engines are in
  the same range (232–384 ms). Type 1's ER-only arrivals show what Reference A's
  pattern is made of. It is a handful of three-pulse groups, each group's
  pulses 1.5 ms apart, at 21.7, 34.7, 49.3 and 69.6 ms. Every group is
  within 0.4 dB of the others (−4.7 to −5.1 dB re the peak), so the
  reflections barely decay across the first 70 ms.
- **Echo density agrees.** At 10 ms, Reference A runs 0.02–0.23 except Plate
  (0.74), and Linger runs 0.02–0.38. Both are sparse, positional clusters,
  which is the Reference-A character the DENSITY bridge's low end was built
  for.
- **Reference A's Decay control is not a T60.** At 1.81 s on the dial, every type
  measures 0.7–1.2 s, full and tail-only alike, on a 5.5 s file that holds
  the whole decay. This matters for M3's calibration: a "1.8 s" matched
  against Reference A by the dial will be twice as long as Reference A really is.
